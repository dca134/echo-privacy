Privacy at a glance

ECHO does not require an account, collect personal information, or send your journal entries to a server.

01 /

Introduction

ECHO is a minimal, atmospheric application designed for emotional reflection. This Privacy Policy explains how ECHO handles information and what happens when you interact with its features.

02 /

Information We Collect

ECHO does not require registration or user accounts. The app does not collect personal information.

03 /

Thoughts and Reflections

Thoughts you enter remain temporarily in the app’s memory during your current session. Journal entries are not saved to persistent storage and are not transmitted to a server.

Temporary information may remain in process memory for some time. ECHO does not guarantee secure erasure of that memory.

04 /

Echo Wall

Echo Wall displays five fictional example reflections. These are not messages written by real users.

Tapping an Echo Wall reflection is an interaction within the app; it does not send a message or establish communication with another person.

05 /

Analytics and Third-Party Services

ECHO does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party software development kits (SDKs).

06 /

Sound and Device Features

ECHO’s sound effects are generated locally on your device. The app does not use the microphone.

07 /

Data Security

Journal entries are kept temporarily in app memory during the current session, rather than saved to persistent storage or sent to a server. However, ECHO does not guarantee secure erasure of temporary process memory.

08 /

Children’s Privacy

ECHO does not collect personal information or require an account. These privacy practices also apply when the app is used by children. Questions about a child’s use of ECHO can be sent to the developer using the contact information below.

09 /

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if ECHO’s features or privacy practices change. Any revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

10 /

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or ECHO’s privacy practices, please contact the developer.
